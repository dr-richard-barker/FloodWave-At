The 2020 question

Roots go under water. Does the shoot know within seconds?

Hypotheses, 2020 deck

A calcium wave moves from the flooded roots up to the shoot.

It then travels along the petioles and across the leaves.

Conclusion, 2020 deck

“Hydathodes appear to be the source of calcium waves in the leaves almost within seconds of flooding the roots.”

Both hypotheses and the conclusion are quoted from the 2020 Flood Wave deck (slides 50, 51 and 61). This deck tests that conclusion against the raw recordings.
