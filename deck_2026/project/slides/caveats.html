Limits of this dataset

What these recordings cannot tell us

One colour, no control line

A single-wavelength reporter brightens for optical reasons too: water under the mesh, focus, leaf movement. No GFP-only plants were imaged.

No flood timestamp

The files never mark when water was poured. Times here count from the start of recording; motion settling stands in for water arrival.

A faint plant

When motion settles the plant sits only 19–71 counts above its surroundings, so amplitudes are uncertain. Timing is the robust measure.

One treatment, one day

All twelve runs are the same water flood, recorded in one session. The 2-week and 3-week groups in the 2020 deck are not recorded.

These limits apply to both the 2020 analysis and this one. They are why the verdict slide says “not seen” rather than “ruled out”: a fast signal below this setup’s sensitivity, or masked by the optical change when water arrives, cannot be excluded.
