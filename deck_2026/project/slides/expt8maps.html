Expt 8, the 2020 “representative” run

The rosette brightens over minutes

Left: margin (orange), inner control band (grey), veins (blue), lamina (aqua). Right: t50, lighter = earlier; white outline = earliest 5%.

Motion settles 48 s into the recording. Half of the plant’s pixels have reached half their rise by 470 s, and the whole-plant signal peaks at 696 s. The earliest 5% of pixels sit at the rosette centre, along petioles and veins, and on leaf margins. Source: results/runs/expt08.
