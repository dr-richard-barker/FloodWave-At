Every pixel, every run, from the raw files

Raw .czi, read-only; 2×2 binned to 41 µm

2

Register

Rigid, to a late reference; flood motion settles first

3

Subtract

Local background from off-plant pixels, checked on held-out blocks

4

Time

t50: when each pixel reaches half its rise

5

Compare

Margin vs an inner control band, veins, lamina

The control band, 80–160 µm inside the edge, tests whether an early margin is real or an edge artifact.

Why t50 and not ΔF/F: the pre-flood baseline is short and overlaps the water arriving, and the plant sits only 19–71 counts above its local background at that point (pooled runs), so ΔF/F magnitudes blow up. t50 depends only on the shape of each pixel’s rise after motion has settled.
