# Data and figure licence

Code in `analysis/` and `scripts/` is under the MIT licence (`LICENSE`).

Everything else is released under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). That covers:
- the raw recordings, when deposited
- the extracted metadata (`metadata/`)
- previews, derived tables, figures and the video clip (`previews/`, `results/`)
- the documentation, and the slide sources of the 2026 deck (`deck_2026/`)

**Exception: third-party material in the 2020 deck.** The 2020 "Flood Wave" deck itself is not distributed with this repository. Only its assessment and two images made from the recordings are in `legacy/2020_Flood_Wave_deck/`. Many of its background slides reproduce figures from other people's work: µXRF element maps, review-article diagrams marked "Adapted from …", eFP browser images, and root-anatomy figures (see `ASSESSMENT.md` §1). Those figures are not covered by this licence and remain with their owners. Only the deck's images and charts made from the 2020 recordings (slides 52–59) fall under CC BY 4.0. The other flooding-section slides also reuse diagrams: slide 62 reproduces a published root-pressure figure, and slides 47, 50, 51 and 61 reuse anatomy or neuron diagrams.

Please cite as described in `CITATION.cff`.
